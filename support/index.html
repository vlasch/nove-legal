Support

Answers to common questions are below. For anything else, email us and we will reply within a few days.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. We will email you a link to set a new one. If you signed up with Apple or Google, use that button to sign in instead.

How do I delete my account?

Open the You tab, tap the gear icon, then Delete account. Your profile, recipes, photos and activity are permanently deleted.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ··· menu on a recipe, make or profile, then choose Report or Block. You will no longer see posts from people you block. We review every report.

How do I filter out allergens?

Go to You → gear icon → Allergies and switch on the ones that apply to you. Recipes containing them are hidden from your feed and search.

Is Nove free?

Yes. Nove is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
