I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. You will get a link to choose a new one. If you signed up with Apple or Google, sign in with that button instead.

How do I delete my account?

Open the You tab, tap the gear icon, then Delete account. Your profile, recipes, photos, and activity are removed permanently.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ··· menu on a recipe, post, or profile and choose Report or Block. Blocked people's content no longer appears for you. We review every report.
