Nove Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 October 2026

Nove is a recipe and cooking app. This policy explains what information Nove collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and clear on purpose.

1. Who we are

Nove is operated by Vladislavs Schalander, the developer of the app. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, you can reach us at vlad@schalander.org.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to work.

Account information. When you create an account we store your email address, your display name, and your chosen handle. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and basic profile details from Google so that we can set up your account.

Content you create. Recipes, photos, comments, and the cooks you post are stored so that they can appear in the app and be shared with other people who use Nove.

Activity. We store the recipes you save, the creators you follow, and the posts you like so that the app can show your collections and shape your feed.

Preferences. Your dietary settings, allergen choices, and any creators you have blocked are stored so that the app can filter content the way you asked.

3. How we use your information

We use your information to run the app, to show your content to other people when you choose to share it, to keep your account secure, and to respond to you when you get in touch. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Where your information is stored

Nove stores your information using Supabase, a hosting provider that manages our database, file storage, and sign in. Supabase keeps the data on secure servers on our behalf. You can read more about their practices on the Supabase website.

5. Payments

Nove is free and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. We do not collect or store any payment details.

6. Your rights

You are in control of your data.

You can edit your profile and your content at any time inside the app.

You can delete your account from the Settings screen. When you do, your profile, recipes, comments, photos, and activity are permanently removed. This cannot be undone.

If you would like a copy of the data we hold about you, send a request to vlad@schalander.org and we will respond within thirty days.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation. These include the right to access, correct, or erase your data, and the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems. Backups that may still contain your data are cleared on a regular schedule.

8. Children

Nove is not intended for children under the age of thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make a meaningful change, we will update the date at the top of this page. Please review it occasionally so that you stay informed.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, email us at vlad@schalander.org.
