A cookbook for iPhone

A cookbook written by the people who cook from it.

Home cooks post the recipes they make most often. Anyone can cook them in a step-by-step mode and post a photo of the result under the original.

In testing Coming to the App Store

How it works

Find a recipe, cook it, post the result.

  1. 01

    Find

    Browse by cuisine, or enter the ingredients you have and see which recipes use them. Allergen filters hide recipes that contain the allergens you select.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Cook Mode shows one step at a time, with the ingredient list in a drawer at the side of the screen. Recipes scale to the number of servings you need.

  3. 03

    Share

    Post a photo of what you made and note any changes. Your post appears under the original recipe, where other cooks can see it.

Makes

See how a recipe turned out before you cook it.

A make is a short post about cooking someone else's recipe: a photo, how hard it was, whether you followed the recipe, and what you changed. Makes sit under the recipe they came from, so you can read other cooks' changes before you start.

Allergens and calories

Know what's in a recipe before you start.

Allergen filter

Pick from the 14 allergens that EU law requires food businesses to declare. Recipes that contain them are hidden from your feed, search and browse. Recipes you saved earlier stay, with a warning.

  • GlutenHidden
  • MilkHidden
  • EggsShown
  • PeanutsHidden
  • SesameShown

Calorie estimate

Nove estimates the calories in each serving from the ingredient list and shows what each ingredient adds. It is an estimate, so treat it as a guide.
