How it works

From someone's kitchen to yours.

  1. 01

    Find

    Browse by cuisine, or type in what's already in your fridge and see what you can make. Allergy filters keep the feed safe for you.

  2. 02

    Cook

    Cook Mode walks you through one step at a time, with the ingredient list a swipe away and portions that scale.

  3. 03

    Share

    Post your make with a photo and your tweaks. It sits under the original recipe, so the next cook learns from yours.
